Fast Company has named Cadence to its 2026 Next Big Things in Tech Awards list, particularly recognizing it as one of the 6 Next Big Things in Computing, Chips, and Foundational Technology . This recognition is an acknowledgment of the work we do every day to help advance the technologies behind modern computing, chips, and intelligent systems. Now in its sixth year, Fast Company's Next Big Things in Tech Awards highlight emerging products, services, features, research, and other technology efforts with the potential to define the future of the industries they serve.
