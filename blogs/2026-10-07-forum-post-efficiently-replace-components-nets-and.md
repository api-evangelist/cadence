Design changes are a normal part of schematic development, whether driven by evolving requirements, component availability, or ECOs. Allegro X System Capture simplifies these updates with the Find and Replace feature ( Edit → Find and Replace ). By selecting Advanced , you can define the scope of changes as Entire Design , Block/Page , or Selection .
