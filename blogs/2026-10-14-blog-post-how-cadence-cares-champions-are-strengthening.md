Earlier this year, the Cadence Giving Foundation (CGF) launched the Cadence Cares Champion program across eight global sites to empower employees to take an active role in creating positive change in their communities. At the heart of the program is a local approach to giving back. Each Champion team brings a unique understanding of the needs, opportunities, and organizations within their community.
