## Site Purpose

Cadence Design Systems is a global leader in electronic systems design, providing
computational software, hardware platforms, and intellectual property used to design
the world’s most advanced semiconductors, electronic systems, and digital twins.

Cadence’s strategy is centered on Intelligent System Design spanning:
- Silicon (ICs, SoCs, chiplets)
- Systems (packages, PCBs, multiphysics)
- Software and AI-driven optimization
